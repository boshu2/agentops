# AgentOps 3.2: the verification keystone

> **3.2 is a minor on top of [3.1](3.1.md) and [3.0](3.0.md).** The north star is unchanged — AgentOps is the in-session operating loop plus the verification membrane that proves work is done. Read [3.0.md](3.0.md) for *what AgentOps is* and [3.1.md](3.1.md) for how it became installable and gate-disciplined. This page covers *what 3.2 adds*: verification becomes the **front door**, the reviewer becomes **standing infrastructure**, the membrane **remembers**, and the CLI **sheds the cathedral**. When this page and 3.0 disagree on the core thesis, 3.0 wins.

3.1 proved the gate; 3.2 makes the gate the product you can hold. Four moves:

1. **`ao verify` is the front door.** One canonical verb over the cross-family review engine — review a change, get a CONFIRMED/REFUTED verdict bound to the provenance ledger, and prove it later.
2. **The reviewer is standing infrastructure.** A warm multi-model service with self-healing routes and honest failure labels, not a hand-rolled per-review invocation.
3. **The membrane remembers.** Catches are recorded, recalled by domain, and honestly triaged; the escape→derived-check loop is proven end-to-end on the shipped binary.
4. **The cathedral is shed.** The default build is the verification + bookkeeping spine; speculative surfaces moved behind build tags or out of the tree entirely.

---

## The verify story (the front door)

Before 3.2, the cross-family review lived in scripts an operator had to know about. Now it is a product surface:

- **`ao verify`** reviews a commit through the pawl engine and binds the verdict to `docs/provenance/ledger.jsonl` — the same ledger the close gate and the receipts read. No verdict = not done, now with a front door.
- **`ao verify init`** installs the portable pre-push verdict ratchet in *any* repo — the membrane is no longer something only this repo has. `ao verify receipts` renders a proof page from the embedded bundle; `ao done` closes a bead only with a verdict reference.
- **`ao verify stats`** prices the loop: dispositions, refute rate, rounds, wall-time, degraded share by reviewer family, and a re-review (rebase-waste) gauge — the instrument for the standing north star of driving the **cost of verified-done** toward the cost of unverified-done.

The waste killer is **REBOUND**: a byte-identical rebase (same patch-id, lineage intact, gate green) re-authorizes an existing verdict instead of burning a fresh cross-family round. Measured re-reviews of unchanged diffs were pure waste; now they are a ledger disposition with Go-side lineage re-validation in the push gate and CI, plus a keep-ref so CI can re-verify an orphaned reviewed commit.

## The standing reviewer (honest degradation everywhere)

The reviewer had a structural single point of failure: cold reviews were codex-only, and a codex outage stalled all validation. 3.2 answers with layers, each honest about what it is:

- A **standing pawl service** (`ao pawl up/down/health/route/metrics`) keeps warm tri-model panes (Claude + Codex + AGY) with capability-adaptive routing, self-healing respawn/reroute, and p50/p95 + agreement SLOs. The default review routes through it when it is up.
- A **cold failover chain** engages only on outage and labels its verdicts `degraded` — the fallback never masquerades as the primary.
- An opt-in **strict two-family cold quorum** refuses to degrade at all: until a second strict-eligible reviewer family is graduated, it reports honest-UNAVAILABLE rather than pretending redundancy exists.
- An **evidence-quality floor** requires a finding-or-attestation plus a genuine-run marker before CONFIRMED counts, and a **live-smoke mode** puts runtime evidence in the packet — the membrane reviews more than the diff.

Reliability fixes landed with the layers: verdicts are what panes actually said (never fabricated from a timeout), all panes are collected (no first-REFUTE guillotine), sends are delivery-based, and cold codex invocations share one hardened stall/echo-defended runner.

## The membrane remembers

3.2 closes the loop the product has claimed since the escape thesis was written:

- **`ao membrane catch`** records a panel catch at the verdict chokepoint with a class key and affected paths; **`ao membrane recall`** retrieves catches by domain and can inject them into pre-work prompts; **`ao membrane triage`** reports recurrence on two honest axes instead of a vanity counter.
- The **EM loop is proven on the shipped binary**: a false CONFIRMED becomes a domain-tagged escape, the escape compiles into a draft constraint, activation enforces it at the gate, the derived check auto-loads by domain citing the escape — and the learned constraint **travels** to CI and clean clones.
- Real token accounting replaced the hardcoded zeros: per-bead capture, `ao yield tokens` across Claude and Codex transcripts, and an SPC governor over the error budget.

What 3.2 deliberately does **not** claim: that the escape corpus compounds (ADR-0011) or that the knowledge corpus is a moat (ADR-0004). Those remain demoted hypotheses — and a new honesty gate mechanically bans unproven-claim language from live docs.

## Shedding the cathedral

The consolidation minor inside the minor:

- **Spine partition.** Fifteen skills carry `spine: true` and a behavioral gate; the default `ao` build ships the verification + bookkeeping core. Corpus-flywheel and legacy families (`harvest`, `refinery`, `mind`, `turn`, `harness`, `operator`, `curate`) build only with `-tags "flywheel legacy"` (ADR-0012). Codex twin regeneration scoped to the spine cut routine regen ~70%.
- **Removals.** The `ao rpi` command surface is gone (ADR-0009 executed; the operating loop is the navigation path). `ao recall` and `ao memory ingest-claude` are gone — recall is consumed from the coexisting open-source memory tools rather than rebuilt in-tree. The bd/Dolt tracker is retired; `br` + `bv` over a git-JSONL ledger are canonical.
- **Skill corpus.** The prune folded 39 sources into 21 targets (105→65), then consolidation reached 63 with `ao skills retire` making retirement deterministic through the dispositions ledger.
- **`ao wiki`.** The OpenKB port compiles `.agents/` into a sanitized gold wiki with verdict-gated, digest-bound publishing — the one knowledge surface that survived the consolidation because it feeds retrieval (`ao lookup --gold`).

## What 3.2 does NOT change

- **The 3.0 thesis stands.** In-session loop, hookless by default, no daemon, no telemetry; state stays in `.agents/` and the ledger.
- **Verification is the proven product; the rest is honestly labeled.** Experimental tiers, demoted claims, and honest-UNAVAILABLE labels are load-bearing, not decoration.
- **Out-of-session orchestration stays a substrate concern** (AgentWorker roles over NTM, with Agent Mail for contested writes and managed agents where selected), not an in-repo daemon.

## Cutting the release

Mechanics are unchanged from 3.1: `ao --version` is injected at build time from the **git tag** via goreleaser ldflags — there is no static version file. The plugin manifests (`.claude-plugin`, `.agy-plugin`, `.codex-plugin`, `images/gemini/plugin.json`) carry `3.2.0` on `main`; the curated notes live at `docs/releases/2026-07-03-v3.2.0-notes.md` and pass the hard notes gate. Cutting the `v3.2.0` tag is the single load-bearing release step: it turns the landed content into a shipped 3.2 and drives the goreleaser release assets + Homebrew tap.

> **Tag-cut status:** the 3.2 content is fully landed and locally gated; the `v3.2.0` annotated tag + push is the one downstream action the release owner performs to publish.
