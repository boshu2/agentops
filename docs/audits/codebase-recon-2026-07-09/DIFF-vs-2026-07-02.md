## Pattern evolution

The July 2 pattern report's central mechanisms remain real: declarative gates,
executable-derived generators, fail-closed decisions, typed exit contracts, and
hash-chained evidence. The current pattern lane adds an important refinement:
four packages independently implement the two-stage hash-chain shape, but they
do not share identical payload semantics. `rpi` includes `prev_hash` inside the
payload hash while provenance and turn-state do not. Any shared kernel must
therefore preserve versioned/legacy codecs and roll out with byte-for-byte
golden chains.

Other newly measured adoption gaps are project-directory resolution, YAML
frontmatter parsing, JSONL scanner limits, shell preamble adoption, Cobra
compatibility facades, and boolean-value normalization. See
[codebase-pattern-extraction.md](codebase-pattern-extraction.md) for the
three-or-more-instance evidence required by that skill.
