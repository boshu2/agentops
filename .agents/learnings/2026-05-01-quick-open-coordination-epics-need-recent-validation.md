# Learning: Open Coordination Epics Need Recent-Work Validation

**Category**: process
**Confidence**: medium

## What We Learned

When a discovery packet creates an open coordination epic with no closed
children, validation should review the completed recent packet instead of
treating the epic as complete.

## Source

Quick capture via `$retro --quick` during validation of `soc-o6eb`.
